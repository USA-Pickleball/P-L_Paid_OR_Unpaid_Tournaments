Model assumptions: today, every player is a paying member and pays full ticket price with no discount. In the scenario, remaining members get the discounts you’ve switched on, lost members who keep playing pay full price as non-members, and new non-members pay full price. Dues are counted per member per year. The ticket price includes the first event; each additional event is charged at the additional event cost. Member discounts apply to the Golden Ticket only, not to additional events. Figures are directional and for planning, not a forecast.
